OpenMAIC (Open Multi-Agent Interactive Classroom) is an ambitious open-source project from the Multi-Agent Interactive Classroom research group at Tsinghua University (THU-MAIC) that lets anyone generate complete, immersive multi-agent learning experiences in a single click. The project reimagines what online education can look like when AI agents don't just assist the instructor — they become the classroom itself, orchestrating presentations, quizzes, interactive exercises, and project-based learning modules from a single prompt. With the v1.0.0 release landing just days ago on August 27, 2026, the platform adds a powerful agent workbench that transforms course creation into a collaborative conversation between human educators and AI.

Built primarily in TypeScript with a Next.js frontend and a robust server-side architecture, OpenMAIC ships as a full-stack platform with 217 branches and 53 tags reflecting its rapid iteration. The star count speaks volumes — 23,938 ⭐ and 4,480 forks, making it one of the most explosively growing educational AI projects on GitHub. Its architecture is provider-neutral by design: you bring your own LLM models, media providers, search backends, and storage layer. The newly published `@openmaic/*` SDK family exposes a programmatic DSL and rendering pipeline, turning OpenMAIC from a monolithic app into an extensible platform others can build on.

The feature set is staggering. The flagship v1.0.0 agent workbench offers a chat-first workspace where an AI agent plans your entire curriculum, builds and revises every page, and works directly from uploaded materials — documents, audio, video, or web search results. Durable server-backed sessions survive restarts with cancel, resume, and steer capabilities. The platform packs 20+ built-in skills spanning slides, quizzes, interactive widgets, project-based learning (PBL), images, voice synthesis, video export, and even `.pptx` import. A one-click MP4 video export turns any classroom into a shareable video. The editor allows direct slide manipulation — drag, resize, rotate, multi-select — plus an "Edit with AI" Pro-mode that uses validated JSON Patch edits with multi-session history.

OpenMAIC's meteoric rise reflects a broader shift toward agentic educational tools that treat learning as a collaborative, interactive experience rather than static content consumption. By open-sourcing the entire platform under MIT license, THU-MAIC is positioning itself to become the "Linux of AI-powered education" — a common substrate that universities, corporate trainers, and independent educators can all adapt and extend. The combination of academic pedigree, production-grade engineering, and permissive licensing makes this one of the most significant education-tech open source launches of the year.
